Privacy Policy

How TUBAL HUB handles information across its website, accounts, community, digital products, and connected services.

Privacy Policy Details.

Last updated: September 25, 2026. This policy describes the categories of information TUBAL HUB may handle and how information may be used when you use the platform and its connected features.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, TUBAL HUB may handle account information such as your name, email address, profile information, authentication identifiers, and information you choose to add to your account. Community features may also handle posts, messages, comments, reactions, profile content, uploaded media, and other information you submit.

2. Authentication

TUBAL HUB uses Firebase Authentication for supported sign-in methods. Authentication providers may process information needed to create, verify, and secure an account. TUBAL HUB does not ask users to submit passwords through public pages.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide accounts and profiles, operate feeds and community features, process user actions, personalize settings, maintain security, prevent abuse, provide support, improve features, and operate the platform.

4. Public and community content

Information you intentionally publish in public or community areas may be visible to other users. Do not post passwords, financial information, private contact details, or other sensitive information in public chats, profiles, comments, feeds, or community areas.

5. Presence and online status

TUBAL HUB may use presence information to show whether an authenticated user is currently online or recently active. Presence records may include a user identifier, display name, profile image reference, online state, and a last-seen timestamp. Presence data is used for real-time availability indicators and related platform functionality.

6. Moderation and safety

To protect the community, TUBAL HUB may process moderation information such as warnings, chat status, mute or ban status, moderation reasons, reports, and related timestamps. This information may be used to enforce platform rules and protect users.

7. Device and technical information

Depending on the service and browser, TUBAL HUB or its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as browser type, device information, IP-related information, timestamps, error information, request information, and security-related signals. Hosting, authentication, security, and other infrastructure providers may automatically process some technical data.

8. Cookies and browser storage

TUBAL HUB may use cookies, local storage, IndexedDB, and related browser technologies for authentication, security, theme preferences, interface settings, notifications, cached application state, and other functionality. See the Cookies Policy for additional information.

9. Firebase and service providers

TUBAL HUB uses third-party infrastructure including Firebase services for functions such as authentication, database storage, hosting-related services, and server-side functionality where configured. These providers may process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

10. Shop, orders, and digital products

Where Shop functionality is used, TUBAL HUB may process information needed to display products, manage carts, record orders, provide digital-product access, and support license or purchase verification. Payment information should be handled by the configured payment provider where applicable. Payment secrets must not be stored in client-side code.

11. AI and automated features

TUBAL HUB may provide AI or automated features. Questions, prompts, or other content entered into those features may be processed to generate a response. Do not enter passwords, payment credentials, or highly sensitive personal information into AI prompts.

12. Links and third-party services

The website may contain links or integrations for social networks, games, news sources, payment services, APIs, analytics, media providers, or other external services. When you interact with an external service, that provider's privacy policy and terms may apply.

13. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, process transactions, comply with legal obligations, or enforce platform terms. Retention periods may vary by data type and service provider.

14. Data security

TUBAL HUB uses reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the features operated. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and report suspicious activity.

15. Your choices and rights

Where available, you may update account or profile information, adjust settings, clear browser storage, stop using a feature, or contact TUBAL HUB about privacy questions. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability. Some information may need to be retained for security, legal, or operational reasons.

16. Children's privacy

TUBAL HUB does not intentionally use the platform to bypass age or consent requirements. Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for a minor to use a particular service, that requirement still applies. Features may also be subject to their own age restrictions.

17. International processing

Because TUBAL HUB relies on internet infrastructure and third-party service providers, information may be processed or stored in countries other than where you live. Applicable safeguards and provider terms may vary by service.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when platform features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

19. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact tubalrr@gmail.com or use the TUBAL HUB Contact page.

Important: This is a general website privacy-policy draft, not legal advice. The final policy should be reviewed against the actual Firebase configuration, hosting, analytics, payment, advertising, AI, age/consent requirements, third-party services, and laws applicable to TUBAL HUB and its users.
